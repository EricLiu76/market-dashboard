# Market Summary Dashboard

**Live page: https://ericliu76.github.io/market-dashboard/**

A daily market summary with two tabs:

- **Markets**: a card for every instrument, grouped into Broad Market Index, Sector Index, Equity Stock and Commodities, with sparklines, range changes, 52-week ranges, signals and a full chart on click.
- **Calendar**: earnings dates in month and agenda views, marked (Confirmed) or (Estimate), with reported results and consensus.

For information only; not investment advice.
