Upcoming events

Recent results

Against the consensus before the report: adjusted EPS (Equity); net income, operating income or EBITDA (Credit). Choose Macro for the latest data releases.

Equity: an upcoming date shows (Confirmed) once the company has announced it, otherwise (Estimate); reported dates show (Confirmed). Consensus covers quarterly and fiscal-year revenue and adjusted EPS, and the beat or miss compares adjusted EPS with consensus. Credit: names under credit coverage; besides revenue and EPS, each has the consensus for its credit metric (net income, operating income or EBITDA), the beat or miss compares that metric, and release times are as the company announced them. Fiscal quarters read 2QFY26: quarter, then the fiscal year as the company names it (Japanese companies by the year it starts). Macro: US and France (FR) data releases (the actual as first published, against the consensus before the release, and the previous figure as revised at the time; Above or Below is not good or bad), FOMC meetings and rate decisions, speeches by the Fed Chair, Governors and New York Fed president, FOMC minutes, Beige Book, policy and political events (elections, budget votes), the US Treasury's refunding, budget and coupon auctions (high yield), EIA crude inventories, the Fed balance sheet, the EIA energy outlook and the USDA WASDE report; for France also the state budget balance and the annual public deficit. Labels start with the country code (US, FR). Macro events sit on their Singapore date, with New York or Paris time alongside; all-day events on their local date. Upcoming lists the next 7 days of Macro events; the calendar keeps them for a rolling year (the past 12 months, and FOMC meetings 12 months ahead). Times are Singapore time, with US daylight saving applied.

Update schedule (Singapore time)

  • 07:30, Tuesday to Saturday: markets data, with the previous weekday's closes.
  • 10:00, Tuesday to Saturday: catch-up run for closes not yet posted at 07:30.
  • 16:00, Tuesday to Saturday: final catch-up run for closes still missing at 10:00.
  • Anything still missing after 16:00 is tried again at the next 07:30 run (after Saturday, on Tuesday).
  • 07:30, every day: the Calendar.

Pending: the market's latest close has not been posted yet, so the dashboard still shows the one before. Provisional: a preliminary close is shown, and the final close replaces it when posted. Weekly release: the series is published once a week, so it can be up to a week behind. Update failed: the run could not update the market; the next run tries again. Each run picks up whatever has been posted by then. This page is rebuilt at every markets run.
